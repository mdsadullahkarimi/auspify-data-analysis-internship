# Netflix Content Rating & Genre Analysis — Business Insights

## 1. Project Overview
This project analyses content ratings, content types, and genre distribution in the Netflix dataset containing 8,790 titles and 10 columns.

## 2. Content Rating Insights
- TV-MA is the most common content rating, with 3,205 titles.
- TV-14 is the second most common rating, with 2,157 titles.
- TV-PG has 861 titles, followed by R with 799 titles.
- These results describe the distribution of ratings in this dataset; they do not directly measure audience popularity or viewing hours.

## 3. Movies vs TV Shows
- The dataset contains 2,062 TV-MA-rated Movies and 1,143 TV-MA-rated TV Shows.
- TV-14 includes 1,427 Movies and 730 TV Shows.
- TV-Y and TV-Y7 appear in both Movies and TV Shows.
- Rating patterns differ by content type, which can support content catalog analysis.

## 4. Genre Insights
Top genre associations:
1. International Movies — 2,752
2. Dramas — 2,426
3. Comedies — 1,674
4. International TV Shows — 1,349
5. Documentaries — 869
6. Action & Adventure — 859
7. TV Dramas — 762
8. Independent Movies — 756
9. Children & Family Movies — 641
10. Romantic Movies — 616

A title can belong to multiple genres, so genre counts overlap.

## 5. Business Recommendations
- Use rating distributions to understand the composition of the content catalog.
- Compare Movies and TV Shows separately when reviewing rating patterns.
- Review genre distribution when planning catalog reporting and content categorisation.
- Combine this analysis with viewing, engagement, and retention data before making audience-demand or investment decisions.

## 6. Limitations
This analysis measures title counts in the provided dataset. It does not establish viewer preferences, profitability, or causal relationships. Results depend on the dataset's coverage and quality.
